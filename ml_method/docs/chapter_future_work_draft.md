# Future work

DRAFT for the thesis. Prose style follows the thesis convention of no semicolons, no
non-mathematical parentheses, and no em-dashes. The directions are ordered by how directly they
follow from the results and the diagnostic.

The first direction is rebuilding the backbone, for example by phasing the polyploid subgenomes so
that ASTRAL sees single-copy lineages. The diagnostic identifies it as the only lever that can lower
the in-distribution mu-distance materially.

The second is training on the conditions the method is meant for. The model was trained on only six
of the configurations, so its results on fractionation and on the other population sizes mix a limit
of the approach with a lack of training data. Fractionated training data can be generated by the
same pruning that produced the fractionation benchmark, without new simulation, and it would also
give a matched split on which to calibrate the threshold of the ploidy-free decode.

Two changes to the decode follow from the results. The ploidy-informed decode fills each species
greedily in order of confidence, and an exact version would select the event set that meets every
ploidy entry exactly with the highest total confidence, which should help on networks whose events
compete for the same copies. An iterative decode, which applies one predicted event, re-extracts
features on the partial reconstruction and predicts the next, would target the nested and
overlapping events where the build residual concentrates, although it needs a model trained on
partial reconstructions.

On the model side, partner accuracy has not saturated with training data, rising from 0.449 at a
tenth of the data to 0.828 at the full set. A gene tree encoder that attends over gene trees could
capture statistics the hand-crafted features miss, although an earlier learned encoder did not beat
them, and hyperparameters could be selected against the reconstruction measures rather than the
detection and partner proxies. The diagnostic bounds all three at 0.025 of mu-distance on
in-distribution networks, so each is most valuable together with a rebuilt backbone.
