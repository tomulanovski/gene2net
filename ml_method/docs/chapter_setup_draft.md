## Training Data

PlaceNet was trained on simulated data, so that the true network is known and the training labels
are exact. The data were generated in two stages. A set of two thousand polyploid networks was
generated once, and gene trees were then simulated from each network under every discordance
configuration, so that the same two thousand topologies are seen under each configuration, exactly
as the benchmark reuses its 21 networks across configurations. The training networks are disjoint
from the benchmark networks.

Each training network was generated by simulating a species tree and grafting polyploidization
events onto it. The species tree is an ultrametric birth-death tree simulated with SimPhy, the
simulator used for the benchmark. The number of taxa was drawn from a weighted set of values between
5 and 80, with about three quarters of the networks falling between 10 and 40, which spans the sizes
of the published polyploid networks. The number of events was drawn from a weighted set of counts up
to 15 and capped at one fewer than the number of taxa. Low counts dominate, two events being the
most common draw at 31 percent, and 6 percent of the networks were drawn with no events at all and
serve as negative examples. Each event picks a target clade uniformly at random and is an
autopolyploidization or an allopolyploidization with equal probability. An allopolyploidization picks
a second parental lineage uniformly at random among the clades not overlapping the target, and where
the target clade holds every taxon no such clade exists and the event becomes an
autopolyploidization. Events were grafted so that the tree remains ultrametric, an
autopolyploidization by attaching an identical copy of the target clade as its sibling and an
allopolyploidization by attaching a copy of it on the branch of the second parental lineage.
Fractionation was not applied at this stage, so the training networks retain both copies of every
duplication.

Gene trees were then simulated from each network under each configuration, the backbone was inferred
from those gene trees with ASTRAL-4, and the true events were mapped onto backbone branches to give
the per-branch training labels. The backbone was re-rooted before feature extraction and labeling,
because ASTRAL returns an unrooted tree and an arbitrary root misaligns the events with the branches
during labeling. A fixed random split, seeded so that it is reproducible outside training, holds out
one fifth of the samples for validation, a sample being one network under one configuration. Every
validation number reported in this chapter comes from that same split, including the hyperparameter
search, the diagnostic analysis and the feature importance ranking, so those numbers are comparable
with one another. The benchmark and fractionation results were measured on the separate benchmark
networks and never use it.

The model was trained on six of the configurations, the three ILS levels and the three duplication
and loss rates at an effective population size of one million. The remaining configurations were
held out entirely, so the results on them test generalization rather than fit.

## Evaluation

Evaluation uses the benchmark of 21 networks, the comparison measures and the baselines of the
preceding chapter. The reticulation descendants and the reticulation sister distances are reported
first, since they ask directly whether the reticulations involve the right lineages and whether
those lineages descend from the right parents, and the μ-distance follows as a summary of overall
structural agreement. Because every node of a network contributes to the μ-distance, it also
reflects the placement of the diploid taxa, which the two reticulation measures do not see.
Completion, the fraction of the benchmark networks a method reconstructs at all, and runtime are
reported alongside the distances.

The baselines are Polyphest and GRAMPAIter. Polyphest requires a ploidy list and was given the list
inferred from the gene trees by the procedure of the preceding chapter, which is the same list
supplied to the ploidy-informed decode, so no method was handed the true ploidy and the comparison
is prior-free on both sides. GRAMPAIter requires no ploidy input and is additionally reported with
the same inferred list supplied as a prior.

The evaluation spans fifteen configurations. Twelve vary the level of discordance, crossing three
ILS levels with four duplication and loss rates, none, low, medium and high. The ILS level is set
through the effective population size, 200,000, one million and two million individuals for low,
medium and high respectively. The remaining three apply fractionation at retention rates of 0.75,
0.50 and 0.25 on top of the medium duplication and loss, medium ILS configuration. Each
configuration was simulated in five replicates, and every reported value is the mean over those
replicates.
